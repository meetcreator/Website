FAQ

Common questions

Straight answers about how we work, architecture ownership, and project deliverables.

What does ROI-driven software actually mean?

Every engagement starts with a financial diagnostic, not a feature wishlist. We identify where your business is losing money or time, and we build software that directly addresses those gaps. The ROI isn't a promise — it's a design constraint.

Do you handle the entire stack in-house?

Yes. Backend (Python, Node.js, PostgreSQL, NoSQL), frontend (React, Next.js), infrastructure (cloud, Docker, CI/CD) — we own the full stack. You work with one team, not a patchwork of vendors.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope, but we work in two-week sprints so you see functional output fast. You're never waiting months to know if something is working — stakeholders can test real modules throughout the build.

What happens after we ship?

We set up automated monitoring, CI/CD pipelines, and thorough documentation so your team can fully own what we built. Ongoing maintenance and scaling support is available — just ask during our initial call.
