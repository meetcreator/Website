Workflow

How it works.

From requisition to purchase order — fully orchestrated.

01

Requisition Intake

Users submit procurement requests via a structured form. The AI classifies category, urgency, and routes the request to the appropriate sourcing pipeline.

02

Autonomous Supplier Sourcing

The LLM engine queries the supplier database, scores candidates on price, reliability, and compliance, and generates a ranked shortlist with justifications.

03

Contract Risk Analysis

Uploaded contracts are parsed and analysed for liability clauses, termination terms, SLA gaps, and pricing anomalies. Red flags are surfaced instantly.

04

HITL Approval Routing

High-value or flagged decisions are routed to designated approvers with full AI context. Approvers accept, reject, or escalate with one click.

05

PO Issuance & Spend Tracking

Approved orders are converted to POs automatically. The spend analytics dashboard tracks budget utilisation, vendor performance, and forecast vs actuals in real time.
